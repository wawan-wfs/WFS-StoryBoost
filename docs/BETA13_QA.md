Current main verified at `cd8ad45535078f80c79876bdde8adb4c11dca378`; beta7 through beta12 drafts already share the ancestry of beta12 `597899ade0ddb1e4e22c73cc35491a8533000fe4`. No open drafts or main were merged or replaced. Both exact-head [pull request](https://github.com/wawanwfs/WFS-StoryBoost/actions/runs/44335579949) and [push](https://github.com/wawanwfs/WFS-StoryBoost/actions/runs/44335437063) runs for beta12 passed all four jobs. Downloaded beta12 reports show 23 passes on API29 and 32 on API35, no failures, and at most 2 px preview-to-decoded boundary deviation for all five caption cases per API.

- Caption painting uses shaped bidirectional runs and preserves paragraph direction across the two display lines. Every supported break is a Unicode grapheme boundary. Regression tests cover Arabic/Latin/CJK and unbroken decomposed accents in decoded AVC; layout checks also cover joined emoji, modifiers and flags.
- The single-clip edited output remains AVC/AAC. PQ and HLG sources use the [Media3 OpenGL tone mapper](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/transformer/supported-formats) before effects and caption, producing an SDR sharing copy on supported devices. Do not retag HDR pixels as SDR or label this output native HDR. HDR clip compatibility depends on decoding and OpenGL, not just the Android API number.
- FFmpeg generates actual 10-bit BT.2020 HEVC Main 10 ramps for HLG and HDR10/PQ. `ffprobe` verifies video tags, depth, AAC, and HDR10 mastering/content light metadata. Android checks input color transfer and native Gallery byte-copy integrity without needing a decoder. Where a Main10 decoder and GL_EXT_YUV_target are advertised, captioned transcode must produce tagged SDR AVC with a decoded luminance ramp and caption, safe margins, AAC timing and unchanged source SHA. Missing capability is a recorded skip, not passing HDR transcode evidence.
- Existing no-caption original copy, rotation, silence, A/V PTS, Gallery cancellation and revoked access gates remain. 24 Color Looks, package and pinned on-device DnCNN are unchanged.
- Owner-only gates remain separate. No physical test handset, Google Photos session, consented images, or pair of independently owner-signed releases was available to certify. See the [Windows runbook](PHYSICAL_ARM64_QA_WINDOWS.md); never commit private inputs or credentials.
